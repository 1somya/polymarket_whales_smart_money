Why “win rate” is a trap

The same wallet — the #1 earner on the platform, up $22M — has wildly different “win rates” depending on the question you ask. Click each question and watch the number move.

—

Same wallet, same bets — 46% to 97% just by changing the definition. That is exactly why ranking wallets by “win rate” is meaningless.

What you’re looking at & the honest limits. 95 wallets that could be fully reconstructed from on-chain fills (≥50 resolved bets each), holding ~21% of the top-500’s realised profit. Clustered on behaviour only — never on profit.

  • Survivorship: the source is the top-500 by PnL — there are no losing wallets here, so this describes species of winner, not what separates winners from losers.
  • Coverage: the biggest concentrated whales (like the #1 wallet) make too few bets to fingerprint and sit outside this 95; wallets first active after 2026-04-28 aren’t reconstructable.
  • The “HFT market maker” label is behavioural (maker-heavy, high-frequency); lum.id’s own classifier defines “market maker” more narrowly.
