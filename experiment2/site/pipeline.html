Why the numbers aren't in file order

The scripts are numbered 10-34, but the cohort (files 20-21) is drawn above Part 1 (10) because it logically comes first: the study began on lum.id's 278 top-winners, then Part 3 replaced that survivorship-biased set with a PnL-neutral cohort sampled from the raw fill tape (6,347 wallets). Part 1 and Part 2 were then re-run over that expanded cohort — so positions.csv and everything downstream reflects the full population (2,055 qualifying wallets after the truncated-history-wallet ruling, 59% losers), not the original winners. The graph shows this logical data flow; the filename prefixes preserve the original authoring order.
