Position-level PnL decomposition

Did the wallet predict or trade?

A wallet buys and sells the same bet many times, then holds whatever's left to settlement. So “did they win?” has no single answer. Part 1 splits every position's profit into the part that came from being right about the outcome and the part that came from trading the price — because only the first is something you could copy.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Imagine a wallet that buys YES at 30¢, buys more at 40¢, sells most of it at 65¢ before the market resolves, and holds a sliver to a resolution that loses.

That wallet was wrong about the outcome and still made money. A leaderboard collapses this into one PnL number and a misleading “win rate.” To decide if a wallet is worth following, we need to know where the money came from:

Holding PnL — profit from tokens carried to the $1 / $0 settlement. A copyable outcome view.
Trading PnL — profit from buying low and selling high before resolution. An execution edge you can't copy.

04 · THE CHECK

The two buckets must balance

This is the validation gate: trading + holding has to equal the raw cash flow — sells, plus the settlement payout, minus buys — for every position. If it doesn't, the matching has a bug.

Across all 888,111 clean resolved positions, the worst imbalance was 8.4 × 10⁻⁷ — floating-point dust. The gate passed.

06 · WHERE THE MONEY CAME FROM

Almost all profit is holding, not trading

Adding up the buckets across the whole cohort, the holding edge dwarfs the trading edge. This is the encouraging headline for a following strategy: the profit is the copyable kind.

holding trading

And per wallet, the same story: a wallet's trading share of profit clusters hard at zero. There is no separate tribe of “traders” — nearly everyone earns by holding.

07 · SCALE & VALIDATION

What Part 1 produced

Edge cases were flagged and counted, never dropped silently: hedged (bought both sides), oversold / CTF-minted (no cost basis), pure maker-sells skipped. These are excluded from the clean metrics and carried as explicit caveats.
